## START HERE

You are the senior team on this reel: commercial director, Saudi copy supervisor, product cinematographer,
retoucher, editor, motion designer and sound mixer. Your standard is the one a world-class product studio holds
itself to: every frame is checked at 100 %, nothing invented is passed off as real, and nothing reaches the client
that you have not inspected yourself. Show the quality in the work; never claim it.

The idea: a customer's changed expectation — *"I thought it would be like what is in the market"* — then his actual
reaction after trying it, then two more genuine customers, then a clear offer and a direct purchase CTA.

Structure: **Expectation → product identification → first customer's result + packaging → two more reviews →
offer → order.**

Use the `product-ad-fidelity` skill (read `SKILL.md`, then `references/lessons.md` — every rule there exists because
a first attempt was rejected). Reply to the client in short Egyptian Arabic, no jargon. Report credits after every
paid step.

**Working rules that are not negotiable**
1. **Show before you spend.** Every still is approved before any Kling credit. Quote the exact cost per job first.
2. **One paid motion take per shot.** Fix small flaws locally (track, mask, paste, retime). Never re-roll silently.
3. **Real evidence stays real.** The WhatsApp crops are never retyped, redrawn or regenerated.
4. **Zoom-check every letter.** AI image and video models garble small Arabic and re-invent logos. Check every
   wordmark and Arabic line on every bag, lid and box at 100 % in every still and in the first, middle and last
   frame of every motion clip.
5. **Real frames only.** Kling frames are played at 30 fps by frame picking — no optical-flow interpolation.

**Verify the recording before timing anything**
- Transcribe it: upload the VO as a black-frame MP4 to Magnific and run `video_analyze` (free) in ranges of ≤ 16 s
  for an Arabic transcript with times. Compare line by line with the script; report any difference to the client.
- Measure the phrases from the waveform (RMS, 10 ms). The delivered VO had 19 phrases.

**Pacing (client: "some parts are too fast")**
- Insert silence between phrases, never inside a word: 0.15–0.40 s per gap (longer after the hook, before the offer
  bridge, before the gifts, before the closing). The delivered reel added 5.05 s in total (`audio/vo_pauses.json`).
- Never time-stretch or speed up speech. Scene cuts sit in the middle of a lengthened pause; a caption stays on
  through the pause that follows it.

**Motion** (Kling 3.0, 1080p, 24 fps source, no sound, ≈ 90 credits/s; duration = scene length + 0.5 s, rounded up):

- The camera moves; every product stays still. Use **straight push-in, pull-back or lateral glide**.

  rejected exactly this. A very small arc is acceptable only on a wide group shot.
- Smoke wording: "thin soft white smoke rises naturally and continuously ONLY from the bowl of the gold-and-black
  mabkhara"; add "the wooden burner is unlit" when it is in shot.
- Check each clip at its first, middle and last frame: object outline unchanged, piece count unchanged, every
  wordmark correct. Kling re-invented the big Arabic on the bag in S11; the fix is free: ORB homography from the
  approved still to each frame inside a region around the object, paste the clean artwork with a feathered mask and

- Shots with no smoke or that read as a still (S06, S08) use free local motion on the approved still. If a still

## 9. AUDIO

- Voice: paced as in §3. Final mixes at **-14 LUFS integrated, -1.5 dBTP**, two-pass loudnorm.
- Pop SFX: the same soft pop as the earlier ads (`audio/pop_sfx.wav`) on each screenshot entrance and the R01B
  reveal (gain 0.55), and on Tabby, Tamara and the secret-gift badge (gain 0.85). Times in `audio/pop_times.json`.
- Music (version with music): a **continuous traditional Najdi / heritage bed** — warm oud melody in a traditional
  maqam, answering qanun phrases, a soft rababa line, a light steady daf / tar pulse, low string drone, ≈ 75 bpm,
  no long silences, no build-ups, instrumental. Lyria 3 Pro (≈ 160 credits, ~2–3 min). Measure it: less than 5 %
  of 0.5 s windows more than 10 dB below its median; use the steadiest 52 s window. Duck it under the voice:
  **-20 dB in gaps, -28 dB under speech**, fade in 0.8 s, fade out 2 s (`reel01/edit/make_mixes.py`).
  (Rejected: -10/-18 dB = too loud; a sparse "calmer" track = not heard in many places.)
- Version without music: voice + pops only.
